# Bille Di Hatti Website Design Brief

Design read: A Delhi food destination where heritage, breakfast energy, and street-side appetite meet in a modern editorial frame.

Concept spine: The first plate. The experience moves from Kamla Nagar context into the visual warmth of a signature North Indian breakfast.

Delivery tier: cinema.

Locked palette: #123B36 deep teal, #E27A63 coral, #A8D5BA seafoam, #F3F6EF soft ivory, #1B252B ink. The palette avoids the usual restaurant red-orange treatment while preserving appetite and contrast.

Locked type: Fraunces for display headlines and DM Sans for navigation/body. The serif carries the heritage/editorial voice; the sans keeps practical actions crisp.

Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous food-focused camera move is scrubbed by scrolling, with the same film also used as the hero loop.

Journey:
1. Arrival: Bille Di Hatti at 72D, Kamla Nagar.
2. Breakfast: Chole poori, bhature and sweet lassi as the visual focus.
3. Menu: Familiar North Indian plates and snacks, with prices intentionally omitted.
4. Review notes: Two verbatim Google-review excerpts from public mirrors.
5. Visit plan: Hours, phone, WhatsApp booking and directions.

World grammar: tactile food photography, deep teal surfaces, soft ivory paper, coral action accents, natural warm highlights, center-safe compositions, subtle grain.

Mobile framing: keep the hero subject in the center-safe zone, use the 720p vertical cut, and stack booking plus directions below the fold.

Section plan: Hero; About; Menu; Reviews; Visit + booking; Footer. Layout families alternate between full-bleed, split, card grid, quote rail, utility panel, and compact contact grid.

Asset plan: 1080p desktop hero loop, 720p mobile hero, exact first-frame posters, storefront image, two food images, favicon family, web manifest, OG image.

CTA inventory: View the menu is a coral action; Book a table is an outlined action; Get directions is an ivory action; WhatsApp booking is a seafoam action.

Anti-convergence ledger: prior build used darker blue/purple/cyan tones and Playfair/DM Sans. This build changes palette family, display face, hero treatment, CTA garments, and corner language.
